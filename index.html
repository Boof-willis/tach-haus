Welcome to Tach Haus

This isn't just a brand // it's a movement. Born from the raw passion of motorsport culture and the gratitude of a life forever changed by charitable giving, Tach Haus lives at the intersection of speed, soul, and significance.

We stand for the purists // the ones who still shift with intent, who feel something deeper when analog meets asphalt. Our gear isn't just swag. It's a badge. For those who know the difference between horsepower and heart. Every piece carries the spirit of the road and the pride of those who live for it. But Tach Haus is more than what we wear or drive.

At its core is The Tach Haus Foundation, our 501(c)(3) committed to giving back to the communities and causes that shaped us. From Shriners Hospitals for Children - who made it possible for one of our founders to walk - to first responders, military families, and others who carry the weight for the rest of us.

We're building something bigger than brand.

A legacy. A rallying point. A reason to move // with purpose.

Charitable Giving Focus

Tach Haus Foundation is committed to channeling its resources toward organizations that create meaningful and lasting impact. The majority of our charitable donations are dedicated to Shriners Children's, an institution that changes lives every day through pediatric specialty care, innovative research, and world-class medical education.

In addition, Tach Haus Foundation supports the 100 Club, an organization devoted to standing behind public safety personnel and their families, providing assistance and relief in times of need.

Through these partnerships, Tach Haus Foundation seeks to honor its roots, serve its community, and ensure that every mile we drive together fuels a greater purpose.

Our flagship experience: a cinematic, curated road rally for true drivers. Named routes. Breathtaking passes. Elevation. Precision. Brotherhood.

REV Rally is not about showing off. It's about showing up - in the machine that moves you, alongside those who get it.

Each Rally supports charitable giving, blending high-performance with high purpose.

Tach Haus

Driven by legacy. Fueled by purpose.

Some stories start in a garage. Ours started in an operating room.

Every year between the ages of 3 to 18, one of our Founders (@andrewlaporte) endured 22 orthopedic surgical procedures at Shriners Hospital for Children in Salt Lake City, UT - a very special place that, along with leading surgeons and specialists who are all the top in their field, afforded him the ability to walk, to move, and to live life fully. As Andrew matured, Motorsports became more than just an interest, it became a passion. It became freedom, and healing, and it forged another bond between Father and Son.

Tach Haus was built on that road. A curated lifestyle brand and a motorsport collective for those who drive with purpose, passion, and a deep respect for the machines that move us.

But beyond the throttle, we believe in giving back.
